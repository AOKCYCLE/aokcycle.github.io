Questions

What kind of projects do you take on?

AI infrastructure and enterprise technology first: GPU servers, clusters, data-centre equipment, networking and storage. I also take on distribution, market-entry, marketplace and gaming-hardware projects.

Can you source specific GPUs or servers?

Yes. Send the model, quantity, destination and timeline, and I'll come back on availability, pricing and lead times through authorised channels, with export-control requirements checked.

Do you work with governments and large enterprises?

Yes. I've led government data-centre and smart-city tenders and work with enterprise and sovereign AI buyers. Confidentiality is standard.

Do you work outside the UAE?

Yes. I work from Dubai with clients, suppliers and partners across the GCC, the US, Asia, Europe and Africa.

What happens after I send an enquiry?

It comes straight to me. I reply within 24 hours to set up a call and, if needed, an NDA before we go into detail.
